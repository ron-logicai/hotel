Welcome to NBA




The NBA began from the growth of basketball in North America: 1. Basketball was invented in 1891 by Canadian teacher James Naismith. He wanted an indoor sport that students could play during winter. 2. In 1946, owners of large ice-hockey arenas created the Basketball Association of America (BAA). They wanted events in their arenas when hockey was not being played. 3. The first BAA game was played on November 1, 1946, between the New York Knickerbockers and Toronto Huskies. New York won 68–66. 4. Another league called the National Basketball League (NBL) already existed. The BAA and NBL competed for teams and talented players. 5. In 1949, the BAA and NBL joined together. The combined league became the National Basketball Association, or NBA.
