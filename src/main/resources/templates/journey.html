Make it to the NBA

Practise dribbling, passing, shooting, defence, footwork, and using both hands.
Play for a local team
Join a school, community, or basketball club. Training alone helps, but organised games teach teamwork and decision-making.
Compete at stronger levels
Try to progress into regional teams, basketball academies, elite youth leagues, or national youth teams. Work on strength, speed, jumping, balance, endurance, nutrition, sleep, and injury prevention.
Get noticed
Perform well in competitive games, attend appropriate camps and showcases, and create game footage. Coaches care more about real game performance than edited highlights.
Choose a development route
Possible routes include:
- American high school and college basketball
- The NBA G League
- A professional league outside the United States
- A recognised basketball academy or development program
Become eligible for the NBA Draft
Players must meet the NBA’s eligibility requirements. Being eligible does not guarantee selection.
Enter the draft or sign with a team
NBA teams may draft eligible players. Undrafted players can still receive opportunities through training camps, two-way contracts, Summer League, or the G League.
